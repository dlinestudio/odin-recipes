Ingredients

  • Rava
  • Ghee
  • Sugar
  • Coconut
  • Elachi
  • Milk

Method

  1. In a pan heat ghee
  2. Add rava and roast in ghee. Rava colour should not change.
  3. When done switch off flame.
  4. Immediately add sugar and coconut and elachi and mix thouroughly.
  5. Add milk and roll it into laddoo
